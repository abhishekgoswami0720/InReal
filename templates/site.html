How the check works

Follow one post from a job site to your list. A post is hidden only when the evidence is in the post itself.

  1. 1
    Collect

    We read the full post from many job sites and career pages, not the two-line preview.

  2. 2
    Check

    Known scam wording, rules for links and contacts, and an AI reviewer that must quote the exact line it objects to. A quote that isn't in the post is thrown out.

  3. 3
    Apply

    What passes reaches your list with the reason it passed, linked to the employer's own post. We never ask you for money.
